## Working Paper

### More is not Necessarily Better: Rethinking Colonial Education and Long-term Development

With **Bingjun Tang** (Harvard University) **Dongzhu Xu** (SOAS University of London)  

This paper studies colonial and pre-colonial education in Manchuria using a geographic regression discontinuity design based on historical provincial borders. Combining archival education data from the 1910s–1945 with outcomes from 1980–2010, it finds that pre-colonial state-led education had stronger effects on literacy and higher education enrollment than colonial initiatives.

## Work in Progress

### Is Agricultural Aid Effective in Promoting Agricultural Growth in Sub-Saharan Africa? A Causal Analysis Based on Remotely Sensed Data

With **Qingqian He** (China University of Political Science and Law)  
*Work in progress*

This project uses remote sensing and GIS to study 20 years of farmland expansion in sub-Saharan Africa and maps agricultural assistance projects over the same period.

### Road to Dispossession? Infrastructure Expansion and Land Grabbing in the Democratic Republic of the Congo

With **Dongzhu Xu** (SOAS University of London)  
*Work in progress*

Building on preliminary fieldwork across 19 villages in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, this project develops a two-dimensional spatial regression discontinuity design and a 600-household survey to study the effects of road paving on land dispossession.
